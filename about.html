Behind the Game

The Concept

Classic Tic-Tac-Toe is a solved game—if both players know what they are doing, it always ends in a draw. MissedTacToe introduces a memory mechanic and a strict piece limit, forcing players to think several moves ahead. The addition of the custom "Expert" AI algorithm ensures that even seasoned players have to stay sharp.
